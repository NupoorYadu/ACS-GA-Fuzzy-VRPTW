### Summary of aggregated experiments

We ran 10 independent repeats (ants=10, iterations=50) for each method and ablation on representative Solomon instances. Aggregated results (mean best cost across repeats) are reported below for the main method ("current") and the baseline (q0=0). Lower cost is better.

| Instance | Current mean cost | Baseline mean cost | Delta (current - baseline) | Rel. change |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| C101 | 1833.40 | 2122.02 | -288.62 | -13.6% (improvement) |
| R101 | 2531.95 | 2521.51 | +10.44 | +0.4% (worse) |
| RC101 | 2512.10 | 2447.63 | +64.47 | +2.6% (worse) |

The aggregate paired statistical tests over the full instance set are reported in the analysis output (see `results/analysis/stat_tests.json`). A two-sided paired t-test and a Wilcoxon signed-rank test returned p-values of approximately 0.062 and 0.069 respectively, indicating the mean improvement is not statistically significant at the 0.05 level for the current experimental setup.

Figures and artifacts were generated by the analysis pipeline and are available under `results/analysis/` (multipage `report.pdf`, boxplots `violin_costs_by_set.png`, mean-cost plot `mean_cost_by_set.png`, novelty radar `radar_by_set.png`, plus per-instance thumbnails). The detailed per-instance aggregated CSVs are in `results/aco_agg/summary.csv` and `results/aco_baseline_agg/summary.csv`.

Interpretation (draft): the proposed ACS variant shows substantial improvements on C-class instances (e.g., C101: ~13.6% lower cost), but performs similarly or slightly worse on some R/RC instances under the current parameterization and run budget. Possible reasons include differences in instance structure (customer clustering vs. mixed/random), sensitivity to q0 annealing and candidate lists, and local search interactions. We recommend adding longer runs (more iterations/ants), per-instance parameter tuning via the GA tuner, and including OR-Tools/LKH baselines for fuller comparison as follow-up work.
